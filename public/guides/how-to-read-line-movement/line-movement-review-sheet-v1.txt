FICTIONAL EXAMPLE
Same-book full-game football spread, matching rules.
9:00 AM America/Chicago: Team A -3.5 (-110), 65% of spread tickets.
11:00 AM America/Chicago: Team A -2.5 (-110), 72% of spread tickets.
Both split observations are from the same book and captured at those times.
Conclusion: reverse-movement pattern observed; cause unknown.
A three-point Team A win loses at -3.5 and wins at -2.5.
These prices and counts are invented for explanation, not actual results.

SEPARATE FICTIONAL TICKETS-VERSUS-MONEY EXAMPLE
70 Team A tickets x $10 = $700.
30 Team B tickets x $50 = $1,500.
100 tickets; $2,200 total.
A: 70% of tickets, 31.8% of money.
B: 30% of tickets, 68.2% of money.
This shows different average ticket sizes, not bettor identities or skill.

Do not raise stakes to chase a signal. Every wager can lose.
