CALCULATIONS
Entry decimal odds E:
Closing decimal odds C:
Opposing closing decimal odds O, if applicable:
Price-based CLV: (E / C - 1) * 100 percent.
Raw probability movement: (1 / C - 1 / E) * 100 percentage points.
Proportional no-vig close p: (1 / C) / ((1 / C) + (1 / O)).
Entry return versus that benchmark: (E * p - 1) * 100 percent.
Examples assume two outcomes with no push outcome and no commission.
Do not apply the two-outcome formula to a three-way market.
Push-capable markets require explicit treatment of pushes.
Do not calculate a price-only ratio across different lines.
Preserve unrounded values for calculations.
