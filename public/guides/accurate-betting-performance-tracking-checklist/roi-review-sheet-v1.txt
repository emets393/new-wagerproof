FICTIONAL CHECK EXAMPLES, NOT RESULTS OR RECOMMENDATIONS
Five 1-unit bets at -200: 3 wins earn 1.5u, 2 losses cost 2u.
Net -0.5u / stake 5u = -10% ROI; win rate 3/5 = 60%.

One 1-unit win at +200 earns 2u; one 4-unit loss costs 4u.
Net -2u / stake 5u = -40% ROI.
Add a 1-unit push: net -2u / stake 6u = -33.33% ROI.
The push does not improve the money result.

Full cash-out: 50u staked, 40u cash returned, no separate fee.
Net -10u / stake 50u = -20% return. If included under the stated policy,
keep this labeled separately from ordinary wins and losses.
If tickets are missing, label the period incomplete and state the
subset used. Never present that subset as a fully reconciled month.
