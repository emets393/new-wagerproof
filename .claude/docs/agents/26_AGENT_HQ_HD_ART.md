# Agent HQ HD art, moods and laptop research (iOS)

> **STATUS: EXPERIMENTAL — parked on branch `agent-hq-fidelity`, NOT on `main`.** Built and tested
> in the simulator on 2026-09-28. The owner hasn't decided whether to ship it. Nothing here exists on
> `main`, and web and Android still use the original sprites.

This branch gives the iOS app higher-fidelity agent characters and an Agent HQ office that feels
more alive:

- **HD characters.** There are 8 new PixelLab characters, one per sprite index. They are used in
  the office and in every `PixelSpriteAvatar`.
- **Office lighting.** There is a night grade, lamp light pools, a monitor glow on agents at their
  desks, and contact shadows.
- **Live wall screen.** The conference-room screen cycles through the roster's working/ready
  counts, record, net units and top agent.
- **Mood expressions.** Avatars blink and smile on standby. Every ~10s they play an expression
  driven by their record, with a pixel icon.
- **Laptop research art.** Each character is seated at a desk behind a laptop, typing, then
  leaning in to think. It replaces the old seated sprite with a laptop layered in front.

Every surface falls back to the original `avatar_N` sheets when an HD sheet is missing.

## Assets

All assets are in `WagerproofKit/Sources/WagerproofDesign/Resources/PixelOffice/`. SPM picks up new
PNGs, so no `xcodegen` step is needed.

| File | Layout | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| `avatar_hd_N.png` | Same 8×9 `PixelAnim` layout as `avatar_N`, 96px cells, feet 10px above the cell bottom. The sit-work rows hold a standing typing loop; row 8 holds celebrate. | `PixelOfficeScene`, `PixelSpriteAvatar` idle |
| `avatar_hd_fx_N.png` | 9 cols × 5 rows, 96px cells, same foot line: standby, happy, celebrate, sigh, facepalm (rows in `AgentEmote` order) | avatar and office emotes |
| `avatar_hd_desk_N.png` | 9 cols × 2 rows, 112px cells: type, research | `AgentDeskSprite` |
| `mood_{sparkle,flame,sweat,cloud}.png` | 32×32 icons | `MoodIconBurst`, office icon pop |

The sheets are generated by `wagerproof-ios-native/scripts/build_hd_agent_sheets.py`
(`python3 scripts/build_hd_agent_sheets.py [indices…]`). It downloads each PixelLab character
export, and the character ids are listed in the script. It then:

- drops wrong-facing frames;
- strips baked-in floor shadows and floor plates;
- paints logos off the laptop lids;
- centre-aligns PixelLab's custom (v3) clips, which render on a larger canvas than the character
  (92 → 104px).

Each PixelLab character has a second state, "Laptop Research" (character 1's usable one is
"Laptop_Research_2"; the script takes the newest).

## Moods

`AgentMood` lives in `PixelSpriteAvatar.swift`. Streaks win over win rate:

| Mood | Rule | Expressions (alternating) | Icon |
|---|---|---|---|
| hot | `currentStreak >= 5` | celebrate, happy | flame, sparkle |
| good | win rate > 50% | happy | sparkle |
| bad | win rate < 50% | sigh | sweat drop |
| cold | `currentStreak <= -5` | facepalm, sigh | cloud, sweat drop |
| neutral | no record | none (standby only) | none |

When `win_rate` is null, the win rate falls back to W ÷ (W + L). The threshold is
`AgentMood.streakThreshold`.

Moods are passed at these avatar call sites:

- `AgentRowCard`
- the followed-agents row in `AgentsView`
- `AgentLeaderboard`
- `AgentIdCard` and `AgentCard`
- `AgentDetailHero`
- `OnboardingLeaderboardPage`

In the office, idle agents facing the camera play the same expressions. They are driven by
`PixelOfficeAgentSpec.winRate` / `currentStreak`.

## Performance rules

A first version redrew every visible avatar 15×/s. It kept the main thread 52% busy, 13% of it
stalled on render surfaces. After the fixes below it is 29% busy with 0% stalls (Debug build,
simulator). Keep these when changing the code:

- **Avatar schedule.** `AvatarSchedule` / `AvatarTiming.tickInterval` wakes the avatar only when
  its picture changes: 2.5/s idle, faster only during an expression or icon. Don't go back to a
  fixed `.periodic` tick.
- **Tile shadows.** They go on the static tile shape, never on the stack containing the animated
  sprite.
- **Office.** `PixelOffice` pauses when scrolled out of view (`onScrollVisibilityChange`). The
  scene runs 60fps only while someone walks, otherwise 30.

## Seeing it (DEBUG screenshot harness)

- `-uiScreenshotMode agentsLoaded -pixel-office-time-mode night|day` shows the Agents tab with
  fixture agents. The harness now selects the Agents tab; before this it rendered a blank office.
- `-uiScreenshotMode agentHeaderShowcase -showcaseAgent <0-4>` shows the research card and the
  live generation card for the chosen fixture agent.

## Known gaps before shipping

- **Desk art varies.** Desk colour and scale differ between characters. The desk sprite is fitted
  to its frame height.
- **Standing typing.** Office typing is standing; PixelLab couldn't produce a convincing seated
  pose without the desk.
- **Other platforms.** Web (`AgentHQ.tsx`) and Android still ship the original sprites. The sheets
  and mood rules would port directly.
- **Unused PixelLab data.** The characters carry unused animations (`sit_type`, a first
  `type`/`type_n` attempt) and character 1 has an unused first laptop state. The builder ignores
  them.
