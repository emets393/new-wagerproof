# CBB — half-life per market

`cbb_market_models.py --stage hl`. One shared feature set, six half-lives, all seven markets graded at fixed selectivity. The panel locked 365d (full game) and 240d (first half) off a sweep that looked only at the full-game spread; the NBA's three markets landed on 180d, 120d and 90–120d within one sport, so a single number serving seven markets is an assumption, not a result.

**Read the shape, not the argmax** — an interior plateau is a result, a lone spike is noise.
