## Verdict

**1. Yes — availability moves the 1H market, and the mispricing is an
OVER-adjustment, not the under-adjustment you would guess.** One moderate
scorer (18–25 ppg) freshly out, opponent healthy, close game → BACK the
depleted team on the 1H spread: **60.9% / +16.0% (n=271, 63/60/60)**. Flat
-110 identical, drop-any-season ≥60.0%, works favored (60.5%) and dog (61.0%),
home (58.2%) and away (63.8%).

**2. It is a dose EFFECT, not a scan artifact — and the sign flips at the top.**
Backing the depleted team on the 1H by points removed: 8-15 → 50.9%, 15-20 →
52.6%, **20-25 → 59.3%**, 25-32 → 52.0%, 32+ → 48.5%. On the FULL GAME the top
of the ladder is where the money is, in the opposite direction: 25-32 ppg
removed → **FADE = 57.2% (n=173, 57/58/56)**, and 61.0% inside close games.
The market over-adjusts a rotation scorer and under-adjusts a genuine star.

**3. The placebo passes.** STALE absences of the same size (same player out,
but out for a while already) in the same close games: 51.2% / -2.4% (n=389).
Only FRESH news is mispriced. Long-term absences are fully in the number.

**4. Full-game TEAM TOTALS price absences dead-on** (round 3, section D):
≥18ppg star out → own team total under 48.9%, over 51.1%, n=689 — the control
with nobody out runs 49.5%/50.4%. 40+ ppg removed → own under 51.8%. So the
book gets "how many points does this team score" right; it gets "how do those
points split across the halves" wrong. Same shape as S7.

**5. Mechanism, measured.** The 1H line is a mechanical 0.573× of the FG line
(regression), and it carries **+0.009** extra for a star out — i.e. nothing
beyond proportional pass-through. Actual 1H margin moves -1.518 for a moderate
absence and only +0.483 for a severe one, while actual FG margin moves -1.018
and +1.697. Absence damage is BACK-LOADED, the proportional 1H line cannot
express that, and the error is biggest where the absence is moderate.

**6. Not claimed.** The 2H column is synthetic — real 2H lines open at halftime
off the actual 1H score, so none of the 2H numbers here are bets. The 32+ ppg
bucket is unstable (1H per-season 70/40/39) and is excluded from both rules.
The gate is games-played ≥25 (first 25 gp = 52.1%): role stats need to settle
before "who is out" means anything.
