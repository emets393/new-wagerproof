## CONCLUSIONS (2026-07-29)

**NBA 1H markets are largely efficient — same verdict as NBA props / movement /
FG (NBA is the sharpest market we study). Two modest TRACK leans, no vault
signal.**

1. **Owner's contrarian hypothesis was BACKWARDS — and that's the finding.**
   Cold favorites do NOT get overcorrected into value; they keep failing.
   FADE a cold-streak (3+ 1H spread fails) FAVORED team in 1H = 54.0%/+3.0%
   (n=363, 56/56/50). Continuation, not mean-reversion. Modest; TRACK-PLUS,
   revisit with more seasons.
2. **Divisional REMATCH → 1H UNDER**: 2nd+ meeting 53.1%/+1.4%, 3rd+ 53.2%/
   +1.7% — but front-loaded in 2023-24 (59→49→52 / 57→50→53). The owner's
   divisional-under instinct is directionally right but weak/decaying. TRACK.
3. **Public-record signals PRICED** (confirms CBB law): hot/cold 1H cover
   streaks, season 1H cover%, season OU% — all ~50% or inconsistent. High-
   cover% back looked +2.8% but 53/47/58 = noise.
4. **Fatigue PRICED**: B2B, favored-on-B2B, rested-dog all ~50-51%. The NBA
   market prices rest thoroughly.
5. **1H ML structural note**: favored teams that "often trail at half" still
   win 1H 58.9% — but priced as big 1H favorites (juice → -7.6%). Remarkably
   stable win rate (59/59/59), not bettable. Home 1H dogs win 36.7% (base).

Bottom line: nothing here rises to a bettable vault signal at NBA-sharp
prices; the fade-cold-favorite 1H lean is the only one worth carrying forward,
and even it is modest. Consistent with the program-wide finding that the NBA
is efficient and CBB is where the roster-driven edges live.
