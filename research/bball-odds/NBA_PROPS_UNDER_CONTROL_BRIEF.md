# NBA props — the under-selection control

The stack's one-sided under-selection was the only positive result in the props programme: positive at CONSENSUS, which no shopping artifact can be. This is the pre-registered control on it.

`delta all` is the original comparator: blind under on the whole population. `delta cell` reweights blind under to the selection's own market x line-bucket mix, and is the number that decides the question. If the model is picking PROPS, `delta cell` survives. If it is picking CELLS where the standing over-shade is strongest, `delta cell` goes to zero and the finding is a restatement of a bias we already knew about.
