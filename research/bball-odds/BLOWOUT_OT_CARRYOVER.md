# NCAAB carry-over: the game after a blowout, and the game after overtime

Study 2026-10-30 (`study_blowout_ot_carryover.py`). Owner question: does a blowout win, a blowout loss, or an overtime game carry into the
next game, in ANY market, and does the gap between games matter.

## Verdict

**Sides are priced.** Full-game spread, first-half spread and both moneylines sit at 50–52% / negative ROI across the whole ladder (won by
15/20/25/30+, lost by 15/20/25/30+, off overtime, won or lost in overtime), every rest gap, home or road, favorite or dog. Backing AND fading
lose at the price. The one lift is a lean, not a signal: a team off overtime playing at HOME is +1.9% on the full-game moneyline (1,436 bets,
5 of 6 seasons positive) against −3.9% for all home teams.

**Totals carry, in three spots.** Records from the bet's side, graded at the consensus close:

| # | spot | bet | record | ROI | seasons | note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | won last game by 20+, then 8+ days off | OVER | 320-248-1 (56.3%) | +7.6% | 6 of 6 | ladder 10+ 53.9% · 15+ 55.7% · 20+ 56.3% · 25+ 55.3% · 30+ 56.2%; 5–7 days rest does nothing (49.3%) |
| A' | lost last game by 20+, then 8+ days off | UNDER | 211-170-5 (55.4%) | +5.7% | 5 of 6 | mirror of A; all games off 8+ days lean over 52.1%, so the previous margin is doing the work |
| B | off overtime, exactly 2 days rest | OVER | 314-240-5 (56.7%) | +8.2% | 5 of 6 | NOT monotone: 1 day 45% (n=115), 3 days 53.0%, 4 days 47.5%. Track, do not trust the shape |
| C | both teams lost last game by 15+ | 1H OVER | 346-268-10 (56.4%) | +7.6% | 3 of 3 | dose-monotone: 20+ 57.9% (n=252), 25+ 62.8% (n=86); the FULL-GAME total goes the other way (under 52–54%) — hot first half, dead second |

A and A' are the clean reads: six-season samples, every season on the right side (A) or five of six (A'), margin ladder flat-to-rising above
15. The 8+ day gap is mostly the December exam break and the holiday week, so in practice these fire in a two-to-three-week window each
season. B is a single-cell peak and C has three seasons of first-half lines; both go in as `tracking`.

**Data fix found on the way:** 268 moneyline prices in `ncaab_games_hist` sat inside (−100, 100) — cross-book medians where books disagreed on
the favorite. They inflated every moneyline payout (first-half favorites read +19% before the fix, −4.5% after). Nulled in the table, guarded
in `export_games_hist.py` and in `ncaab_team_trends_daily.py`.

Universe: every Division I team-game 2020-21 → 2025-26 whose previous game was in the same season, next game regular season (`game_type = STD`), graded at the consensus close in `ncaab_games_hist`. First-half and team-total lines exist from 2023-24. Overtime from CBBD period scores (`game_ot_ncaab`).
