## 🔨 THE HAMMER INDEX — how CFB coaches handle big leads

We studied **97 FBS head coaches** across 5 seasons (2021–2025, 768k plays, garbage time INCLUDED — that's the whole point) to answer one question:

**When they're up 28 on a cupcake, who keeps swinging — and who calls off the dogs?**

Every coach is measured against his OWN normal-game baseline: does he keep the tempo up, keep throwing, leave the starting QB in, keep scoring after +21?

**⬆️ THE HAMMERS**
```
Jedd Fisch      (Washington)  +1.06
Curt Cignetti   (Indiana)     +1.02
Jim Mora        (Colo State)  +0.96
Gus Malzahn                   +0.84
Lane Kiffin     (LSU)         +0.83
```
Cignetti is the archetype: 100 big-lead drives, still scoring **4.16 pts/drive**, 75% go rate on 4th-and-short, and a median of **+14 MORE points** after reaching +21. Early season vs cupcakes? **+35**. Up 21 on a small school, Indiana wins by ~56.

**⬇️ THE MERCY TIER**
```
Chuck Martin    (Miami OH)    -1.67
Tim Albin       (Charlotte)   -1.11
Jeff Traylor    (UTSA)        -1.06
Kalani Sitake   (BYU)         -0.80
Kirk Ferentz    (Iowa)        -0.69
```
Ferentz is the purest example in football: up 21+, Iowa's median margin expansion is **literally ZERO**. The score when they hit +21 is the final score. Book it.

The big brands? All mid-pack — Day, Smart, Swinney, Sark all within a whisper of average. Coach identity lives in the tails.

## 💰 Why it matters for betting

We tested it honestly — each season graded only by the OTHER four seasons' coach ratings, versus real closing lines.

**Weeks 1–3, favorites of 14+ points:**
```
HAMMER-tier coach favs:  59.0% cover  (n=61)
Mid tier:                50.8%        (n=120)
MERCY-tier coach favs:   46.8% cover  (n=62)
```
The hammer side never dipped below 50% in any of the 5 seasons. Early September, the market prices the roster — it doesn't price whether the coach will KEEP scoring in the 4th quarter of a 45-10 game. That's exactly when big spreads come down to style points.

⚠️ Honesty note: the "fade mercy coaches" side flipped regimes (it actually covered in '21–'22), so we're not touching it. The hammer angle goes into **paper tracking for 2026** — if it holds live, you'll see it as a signal in the app.
